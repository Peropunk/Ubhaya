# UBHAYA

## 1. A claim should open its source

The synthetic PC-14 case asks what the records support about a file copy.
UBHAYA preserves source bytes, hashes each artifact and points findings to
the original row, line or frame.

Presenter cue · Show the loaded case and an artifact hash.

## 2. Follow one claim

Open “The files were copied around 22:15.” The PC log shows USB and copy
records. PC-14 row 22 shows 16:44:05 UTC, which becomes 22:14:05 IST with a
one-minute range on either side.

Presenter cue · Click the finding and highlight the exact source row.

components. The telecom draft leaves the subscriber identifier for

### Q&A

- **Does a login identify the person at the keyboard?** The finding stays open because the log identifies account activity and the other records also have limits.
- **What happens if the camera text is unreadable?** The reviewer can open the original still and enter a time with an audit entry if it can be confirmed.
- **Can we bring the prototype to the event?** The team needs the organiser’s written answer.
