The cached demo has 18 adjudicated claims and 276 parsed events before any
manual time entry. The two clear camera overlays yielded timestamps. The
blurry still required manual entry. The report records the synthetic review
rejecting the repeated V-03 scan as a possible double tap.

All six original live extraction trials timed out. Three further trials
with a shorter prompt also timed out. The fallback
scores describe the bundled annotations and deterministic verdict engine.
Organizer approval, mentor feedback, team rehearsals and the presenting
laptop's physical Wi-Fi-off check require their respective participants.
